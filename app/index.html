    How Fearless Draft works

    In a Fearless Draft series, any champion picked in an earlier game is locked for the rest of the series. Bans reset every game. As the series goes on, the available pool shrinks — especially in roles with fewer champions — so coaches must plan several games ahead.

    Draft order is the standard pro sequence: 6 bans, 6 picks, 4 bans, 4 picks. Click a champion in the pool to place it in the current slot. Click a filled slot to clear it (only the most recent action can be undone).
